5 HTML Tags and Their Descriptions

  1. h1- used to make the most important heading text on the page. The opening tag is placed before the text we wish to be the most important, and the closer placed directly after. Name: Heading 1
  2. div- used to maintain order within the HTML code. It shows the content on a different line. Name: Division
  3. body- used to represent what will be visable to the viewer. the opening tag is placed after the head but before the content. the closing tag will be after all the content but before the footer. Name: Body
  4. ol- used to make and ordered or numbered list. The opening tag is placed before the li tags each representing one list item. the closing will be after the last li tag. Name: Ordered List.
  5. a- used for creating a link on a webpage. The opening tag is used before the href tag and the closing will be after what the link will be displayed as. Name: Anchor.
